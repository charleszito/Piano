How to run the test

Stand the phone on the music rest or lay it on the keyboard's top panel, screen facing you, roughly level with middle C. Turn off fans and anything humming if you can.

  1. Tap Start listening and allow the microphone. Then tap Measure the room and stay quiet for two seconds — that sets the noise floor.
  2. Play middle C alone, firmly, and let it ring. The big letter should read C4 and hold steady, not flicker between notes.
  3. Play C D E F G A B C slowly, one note per second, right hand from middle C. The strip should read those eight notes in order, with nothing extra.
  4. Now play the same run quietly, then fast. Where it starts dropping or inventing notes is the real limit we design around.
  5. Play C and E together, then C E G. Expect it to name only one of them — that's normal here and tells me how much work Stage 3 needs.

Tell me what happened at each step and I'll tune the detector, or change the plan if the mic can't carry it.
